YouTube's in-channel search only matches words in titles. You cannot ask it for videos under twenty minutes, or the ones you started and never finished, or a big channel's best work. This adds all of that.

Open any channel's Videos tab and press the Search+ button. The extension reads the channel's entire upload history, then replaces the grid with one you can actually query. Free, with no paid tier and nothing held back.

SEARCH
- Length, view count, upload date, and keyword, combined freely
- Watch state: hide what you have finished, or find what you abandoned
- Free time: pick the minutes you have, see only what fits
- Start here: a spread of a channel's best work for when you arrive cold
- Sort by views per day, which surfaces breakouts rather than just old uploads

INSIGHTS
- Where a channel's sweet spot is, when the data actually supports one
- Uploads per year, and which years the channel performed best in
- View and length distributions, clickable to filter the grid
- Which title formats this audience responds to: questions, versus, how-to
- Compare any two channels side by side
- Keep a watchlist and see what is outperforming across all of them

Most in-page filter tools only touch the video cards already loaded on screen. This reads the channel's complete catalogue first, so every sort, statistic and chart covers all of it rather than just what you scrolled past.

Everything runs in your browser. No account, no API key, no server, and nothing is ever sent anywhere. Full source is published.
