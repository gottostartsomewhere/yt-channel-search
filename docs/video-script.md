# Needle promo video: one take

One continuous screen recording, no cuts, no voice. About 3 to 4 minutes.
Each step flows into the next, so the whole thing is one path through YouTube.

## Before you hit record

- Record with the local build in `dist\chrome` (2.0.1), not the store copy, so
  YouTube's chips work with the panel open.
- Signed in, dark theme, 1920x1080, Chrome maximised, zoom 110%, bookmarks bar
  hidden (Ctrl+Shift+B), notifications off.
- The day before: follow `rust <20m` (or your demo search) so the search bar
  shows "N new" and the toolbar icon has a number.
- A few hours before: "Index channels you watch" ON in the popup, so
  `in:channels` has something to search.
- Just before: search `rust` and scroll the results once (gives `is:fresh`
  something to hide), and open your demo channel, history and Watch Later
  with the panel once each, so they load instantly from cache on camera.
- Use searches and a channel you are fine showing publicly.
- Move slowly, pause about a second after every result appears. If you slip,
  keep going; only trim the start and end.

## The route

1. **Home page.** Click the empty search bar. The "N new" from your followed
   search, "Pick up where you left off", Watch Later, and the tips row show.
2. Click the "N new" row. The results open with the new videos marked NEW.
3. Click the search bar again, clear it, and type `rust`. Your Watch Later and
   history matches appear above YouTube's suggestions, words underlined.
4. Arrow down to one, then Escape instead of opening it.
5. Clear the bar and click the `<20m` tip. It drops into the bar. Finish the
   search: `rust <20m >100k date:month`, press Enter.
6. **Results page.** The panel opens with those filters already set as chips.
   Add "+ Watched" → Unwatched, "+ Free time" → 30 min. Change Sort → Newest
   upload. Watch the count drop.
7. Scroll to the bottom of the panel, click Load more.
8. Scroll back up, click Follow. Then click Close.
9. On YouTube's own results: hover a thumbnail, click the mute button. Show the
   toast, then click Undo. Pan slowly over the "Watched" / "In Watch Later"
   tags on the thumbnails.
10. Search `rust is:fresh`. The results you scrolled before are gone.
11. Click a channel name on one of the results, go to its **Videos** tab.
12. Click the needle button (bottom right). It reads the whole channel.
13. Type a word in the panel, "+ Length" → 20-60 min. "+ Views" → Custom… →
    10k to 1m → Apply. Sort → Most views, then Start here.
14. Press Alt+Y (panel closes), Alt+Y again (it opens). Then click the toolbar
    icon → "Open on this channel" to show the popup can do it too.
15. Insights tab: hover the charts, click a length bar (the grid filters).
    Compare: type another channel's handle. Watchlist: add one, refresh.
16. Back to the Search tab, click CSV to show the export.
17. **History** (left sidebar). Needle button. Watched → Not finished, then type
    `watched:august` in the panel.
18. **Watch Later** (left sidebar). Needle button. Free time → 20 min.
19. In YouTube's search bar: `in:wl >30m`, Enter (panel on your Watch Later).
    Then `in:channels` plus a word, Enter.
20. Then `@` a channel plus a word, Enter (only that channel).
21. Open the toolbar popup and scroll slowly: the cheat sheet, the switches,
    followed searches, muted channels.
22. End on the home page with the empty search bar clicked, dropdown open.
    Hold 3 seconds, stop recording.

## For the listing and Reddit

Upload the full take to YouTube as unlisted and put that link in the store's
video field. For Reddit, trim a 45-second piece out of steps 1 to 6: the search
bar is the part nobody else has.
