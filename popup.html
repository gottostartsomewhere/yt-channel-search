New in 2.3

  • Mute from the ⋮ menu. Every video's ⋮ menu now has Mute channel everywhere, next to YouTube's own options. Unlike "Don't recommend channel", it reaches search too.
  • Shorts go too. A muted channel's Shorts now leave Home and search as well, not just its videos.
  • Fixed: the mute button on thumbnails. YouTube's hover preview covered it, so it only ever worked in Needle's panel. The ⋮ menu replaces it.
  • Fixed: typing a filter like <12m. It waited for a space or Enter and showed an empty list meanwhile. Now it applies as soon as you stop typing.
  • Fixed: playlist length out of sight. On playlist pages, Watch Later and Liked included, the panel's header covered the line with the total length. It's back in view, and the header stays in reach again as you scroll a channel or a playlist.

The panel

Searches you follow

    Type a search with a filter, like rust <20m, and press Follow in the dropdown or the panel. New matches wait for you in the search bar.

    Muted channels

      Open the ⋮ menu on any video and choose Mute channel everywhere. A muted channel leaves search, Home, Subscriptions and the videos next to the one you're watching, Shorts included.

      Muted words

        A video goes when its title has the word, as a whole word: muting AI leaves "said" alone.
