-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 NAME
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

 Needle for YouTube: Channel Search, Watch History & Search Filters

 Renamed at 2.0.0 from "Channel Search+ for YouTube", once the search bar made
 the extension about all of YouTube rather than one channel. The words
 "Channel Search" stay in the title on purpose: the old listing ranked #7 for
 that query, and AI assistants will keep sending people who search the store
 for the old name. Both still land here. The extension ID, installs and
 reviews carry over; only the name changes. The Firefox add-on ID stays
 channel-search-plus@johnkevin.dev, because changing it makes a new add-on.

 Not "YouTube Needle". Google's branding rules do not allow a product name
 that begins with YouTube. The "X for YouTube" form is the accepted one.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 SHORT DESCRIPTION            132 char limit
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

 The YouTube search that knows what you've watched. Channel search, history
 and Watch Later, filtered by length, views and date.

 (127 characters. Must match the manifest description exactly, and it is one
 line in the dashboard: wrapped here only to fit this file.)

 Changed at 2.0.0. Leads with the one thing neither competitor does: YouTube
 Search Plus searches YouTube without knowing what you watched, and Recall for
 YouTube knows your history but cannot search YouTube. "Channel search" is
 still in it verbatim, for the reason below.

 Changed at 1.1.0, when watch history shipped. "channel search" is kept
 verbatim because the listing ranks #7 on that query in store search and the
 phrase is load-bearing. "watch history" is added rather than traded for it, so
 a new term is gained without risking the one that already works.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 DETAILED DESCRIPTION
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

 DO NOT PASTE FROM THIS FILE. Paste from docs/store-description.txt.

 The copy below is hard-wrapped at 72 characters so it reads as a plain text
 file. The store preserves newlines, so pasting it verbatim produces short
 ragged lines with a wide empty margin down the right. docs/store-description.txt
 holds the same words with each paragraph on a single unbroken line, which lets
 the store flow the text to its own column width. Bullets stay one per line in
 both, because those breaks are wanted.

YouTube's in-channel search only matches words in titles. You cannot ask it
for videos under twenty minutes, or the ones you started and never finished,
or a big channel's best work. This adds all of that.

Open any channel's Videos tab and press the Search+ button. The extension
reads the channel's entire upload history, then replaces the grid with one you
can actually query. Free, with no paid tier and nothing held back.

SEARCH A CHANNEL
- Length, view count, upload date, and keyword, combined freely
- Watch state: hide what you have finished, or find what you abandoned
- Free time: pick the minutes you have, see only what fits
- Start here: a spread of a channel's best work for when you arrive cold
- Sort by views per day, which surfaces breakouts rather than just old uploads

SEARCH YOUR WATCH HISTORY
The same panel opens on your Watch history page, where YouTube gives you a
title search and nothing else. No way to sort it, no way to narrow it, no way
to find the one you want.
- Everything you started and never finished, which nothing else will show you
- Filter your history by channel, length, views or when you watched it
- Sort by most recently watched, or work backwards from the oldest
- Find that video you know you watched, without scrolling for it

INSIGHTS
- Where a channel's sweet spot is, when the data actually supports one
- Uploads per year, and which years the channel performed best in
- View and length distributions, clickable to filter the grid
- Which title formats this audience responds to: questions, versus, how-to
- Compare any two channels side by side
- Keep a watchlist and see what is outperforming across all of them

Most in-page filter tools only touch the video cards already loaded on screen.
This reads the whole list first, whether that is a channel's complete
catalogue or your history, so every sort, statistic and chart covers all of it
rather than just what you scrolled past.

Everything runs in your browser. No account, no API key, no server, and
nothing is ever sent anywhere. Full source is published, so every claim here
can be checked against the code.

Reading your history beyond its first page is off by default and stays off
unless you turn it on. It is explained in full in the privacy policy before
you decide.

Not affiliated with YouTube or Google.

 Store search is locale-aware, so a listing carrying Portuguese text can match
 Portuguese queries in a way an English-only one cannot. The problem is
 universal too: YouTube's channel search is equally weak in every language.

 Four rather than one. The earlier plan was one language, measured, then
 expand. That was too cautious: a language costs an hour of listing work with
 no package change and no review risk, a weak Spanish listing cannot damage the
 English one, and the dashboard reports installs BY COUNTRY, so each language
 can be attributed on its own without staggering them.

 NAME       Needle for YouTube: Channel Search, Watch History & Search Filters
            unchanged in every locale

 Not translated anywhere. It is a brand, Google's rule against a name beginning
 with YouTube still applies, and around 36% of traffic arrives from AI
 assistants having been told the English name. Somebody searching the store for
 what ChatGPT called it has to find it.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 PERMISSION JUSTIFICATIONS      the dashboard asks for each one
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

 storage
   Stores the user's own settings, such as which sort the panel opens on and
   how deep to read a channel, so they persist between sessions. No browsing
   data is placed in sync storage.

 Host permission for www.youtube.com
   The extension's entire function is to read a YouTube channel's public video
   listings and render a filtered view of them onto that page. It requests the
   same listing endpoints the site itself uses, and it draws its interface into
   the channel page. It runs on no other site.

 Remote code
   None. All code is contained in the package. No scripts are fetched or
   evaluated at runtime.

 Data usage disclosures, tick these

                               the page and kept on the device
   [ ] Personally identifiable information
   [ ] Health, financial, authentication, personal communications, location,
       user activity beyond the above, web history

   And certify:
   [x] Not being sold to third parties
   [x] Not used or transferred for purposes unrelated to the single purpose

 SINGLE PURPOSE STATEMENT
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

 Searching and filtering the videos of a YouTube channel.

 Every feature serves that: the filters narrow a channel's catalogue, the charts

 another. Keep the framing tight. Review objects to extensions that read as a
 bundle of unrelated tools.

 CAPTURING AT EXACTLY 1280x800
 Ctrl+Shift+M for the device toolbar, set a custom 1280 x 800, collapse
 YouTube's sidebar with the hamburger so the panel gets full width, then
 Ctrl+Shift+P and run "Capture screenshot". That writes exactly those pixels
 with no browser chrome in the image. Below about 1180 the filter row wraps to
 two lines and the shot looks cramped.

 The store takes PNG or JPEG at 1280x800 or 640x400. DevTools writes PNG, so
 there is nothing to convert.
